Inventory

Warehouses, stock items, the stock ledger (receipts, adjustments, transfers), recipes and sales consumption, and Step 5: units of measurement, variant attributes and variants, bills of materials and production orders, under one Inventory sidebar group. Code: apps/api/src/modules/inventory/, apps/web/components/inventory/, apps/web/lib/inventory/, apps/web/lib/nav/inventory-pages.ts. Spec: docs/modules/inventory.md (Steps 1, 2, 5). Decisions: ADR 0027, ADR 0028, ADR 0037, ADR 0038. Branch: feature/inventory-extension (on top of feature/crud-permissions).

Web

  • Sidebar: Inventory opens and closes a list of its pages; it opens by itself on an Inventory page and marks the current one. Each page shows only with its feature and key.
  • Stock (/inventory): tabs Stock, Receive, Adjust, Transfer, Documents, Items, Warehouses. Receive lines offer the units of the item's unit (doc-unit) and show "1 cs = 24 pc"; parents with variants are not offered on any document.
  • Units of Measurement (units-page.tsx): list, add, edit (base unit locked), archive.
  • Variant Attributes (attributes-page.tsx): values comma-separated, checked for empty, duplicates, length.
  • Variant Products (variants-page.tsx): items with their variants; Generate variants with attribute and value checkboxes, live combination count, article number prefix.
  • Bills of Materials (boms-page.tsx): card per bill; editor with output quantity and ingredient lines; delete behind a confirmation.
  • Production Order (production-page.tsx): warehouse, item, quantity; preview of ingredients needed vs on hand (shortages marked); recent orders.
  • Exact decimals in the browser: lib/inventory/format.ts (scaledQuantity, compareQuantities), never JS numbers.

Known gaps and notes

  • API e2e and the migration drift check were not run: the development laptop has no Docker. Run them before merging.
  • Production is one step (no drafts or in-progress state); a correction is an adjustment (ADR 0038 §5).
  • Variants are stock items; menu sizes with their own prices stay in Catalog.
  • Products and Product Category open the one catalog page; the catalog's "New product" button still shows for members without catalog.products.create (ADR 0037 follow-up).
  • Unrelated, already failing on this branch: components/tables/tables.test.tsx (timeouts) and lib/audit/diff.test.ts (no labels for floorX, floorY).
