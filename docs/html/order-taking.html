Technical flow

An item with a seat

  1. TillSeat field on each cart line (optional, 1–20; anything else is not sent).
  2. Till → APIPOST /pos/tables/:tableId/tickets { lines: [{ productId, quantity, note?, seat? }] }, permission pos.orders.take.
  3. APIStores pos_order_lines.seat. It never changes; a correction is void and send again.
  4. LaterA void and both lines of a transfer copy the seat; a merge leaves lines as they are.

Bill by seat

  1. TillSplit bill → By item → Choose a seat.
  2. BrowserFills the quantities of the unbilled items of that seat.
  3. Till → APIThe normal POST /pos/bills with those line ids. Bills know nothing about seats.

The app on a phone

  1. PhoneAdd to home screen. One manifest for everyone, in the partner's name and colors.
  2. StartThe home page. People with the Overview stay there; others who may read orders go on to /service, only when opened from the home screen.
  3. Service workerKeeps /_next/static/ files on the device and shows push messages. Never pages, api answers or sign-in state.

Known gaps

  • The cart has one line per product: two soups for two seats need two sends.
  • The seat is not on kitchen tickets or the kitchen screen.
  • The seat field and the seat picker have no web test.
  • The static-file cache keeps its name across releases, so old files stay on the device until the browser clears them.
