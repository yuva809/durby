Reports and metrics

Sales, tables and reservation reports with CSV export (ADR 0081). Code: apps/api/src/modules/reports/, apps/web/components/reports/, packages/shared/src/metrics.ts. Spec: docs/modules/reports.md.

  • A sale is what was sent from the till minus voids and discounts, counted when ordered. It is not accounting revenue; Durby records no payments yet.
  • A business day starts at 04:00 (Settings → Business day starts at), in each location's own time zone.

Technical flow

From the till to a figure

  1. pos, reservationsA send, void, move, merge, discount, close or booking change writes an outbox event in its own transaction.
  2. WorkerReportsProjectionHandler locks the order (advisory lock) and asks pos for the whole order through PosSalesReader.
  3. WorkerReplaces that order's rows in report_order_facts and report_line_facts. It never adds up, so late, repeated or reordered events end the same.
  4. APIA report is one read-only snapshot over the fact tables, narrowed to the caller's locations. Every figure comes from one function per metric (metrics.ts).
  5. BrowserPeriod and location are in the address; CSV is built from what is on the page.

Past orders: rebuild

Needed once per organization after the first release, and after switching reports on. Safe while the till is in use; batches of 100, the same code as the handlers.

cd /opt/durby
bin/dc run --rm worker node dist/cli/reports.js rebuild --org <organization id>

Known gaps

  • Merged tables count as two orders, which lowers the average order and the second-round rate a little.
  • Items moved to another table count at the time of the move. Totals don't change.
  • Category shares use the product's category now.
  • "Least sold" lists products sold at least once.
  • The location filter lists all active locations; a limited manager who picks another one gets "Location not found".
  • The distributor menu has no Reports item (it has no till).
