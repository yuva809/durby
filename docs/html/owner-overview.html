Owner overview

The home page for owners, admins and managers: all locations today, and yesterday against a normal day (ADR 0082). Code: apps/api/src/modules/reports/reports.service.ts, day-figures.ts, apps/web/components/reports/overview.tsx. Spec: docs/modules/reports.md → Overview.

What it shows

PartContent
Todaysales, orders, average order, open tables, change against the same weekday last week up to the same time
Yesterdaysales against a normal day: the average of the 4 same weekdays before, without days that had no orders
Locationsthe same per location, with "From 3 of 4 days"; one location shows it in the card
No comparison"No comparison yet", never 0 %
No data"No sales yet. Earlier orders appear once the report data has been built."

For people with reports.sales.read in organizations with reports and pos. Everyone else keeps the greeting. The start page setting, branch and driver redirects come first; in the installed app waiters go on to Service.

Technical flow

  1. BrowserThe home page waits for the settings (start page), then calls GET /reports/overview.
  2. APILocations the caller may read: active, no warehouse sites, by name. One read-only snapshot.
  3. APIToday and last week from the fact tables; open tables live from PosFloorReader.
  4. DayFiguresYesterday and the four same weekdays, each a whole business day of that location. Shared with the daily push.
  5. APIRounded per location first; the total is the sum of what is listed and compares only locations that have a normal day (leftOut says how many don't).
  6. BrowserAgain every 60 s while visible. If that fails for a moment, the last figures stay with their time and a notice.

Known gaps

  • Holidays are not known: a closed day yesterday shows as −100 %.
  • Archived locations are left out here but not in Reports, so a period there can be higher.
  • Until rebuild has run, normal days come from fewer days or don't exist.
