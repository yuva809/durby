Data model

Every table is tenant-scoped (RLS, FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY, tenant_isolation policy) and append-only: durby_app has no UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE, and a statement trigger <table>_append_only blocks the owner too. A catalog test fails if a trigger is dropped.

 ├─ PosBillOrder           pos_bill_orders           orders the bill covers (1–10, one location)
 ├─ PosBillItem            pos_bill_items            billed portions: name, unit price, rate,
 │                                                   quantity, discount_amount (≤ 0)
 ├─ PosGuestCheck          pos_guest_checks          number CHK-{YYYY}-{seq:6}, share_index 0 or 1..n
 │   └─ PosGuestCheckShare pos_guest_check_shares    a share's gross per VAT rate

 └─ PosOrderDiscountLine   pos_order_discount_lines  amount per line, negative, at the line's rate

Migrations: 20261112100000_pos_guest_checks (tables, print job check_id, per-check open-job index), 20261113100000_pos_order_discounts (discount tables, discount_amount). Both expand-only.

Number series pos.guestCheck (feature pos, prefix CHK, yearly). Audit actions pos.bill.created, pos.bill.cancelled, pos.discount.added, pos.discount.voided; snapshots hold ids, amounts and kinds, never reasons or cancel reasons.

Web

  • Till (components/pos/order-page.tsx): Print bill bills what is unbilled and prints the new check; with nothing unbilled it reprints the newest check (Reprint bill). The order panel lists bills with reprint buttons, discounts with Remove, and the unbilled amount.
  • Split dialog (split-dialog.tsx): modes by item, equally (2–20 guests), merge (other open tables at the location, loaded from the floor).
  • Discount dialog (discount-dialog.tsx): target (whole order or one item), percent or amount, value, reason from five built-in options. Shown only with pos.orders.discount.
  • Checkout (/checkout, checkout-page.tsx): tabs Open tables, Recent checks, History. A check opens as its receipt; Reprint keeps the number; Cancel bill needs pos.bills.cancel.
  • Receipt (check-receipt.tsx → receipt.tsx): shows "Share n of m", discount lines and a CANCELLED stamp.
  • Printing: bill jobs carry a checkId; the Android till draws that check (print-job-runner.tsx).

Permissions: pos.bills.print (admin, manager, waiter, cashier); pos.bills.cancel and pos.orders.discount (admin, manager; owner has all).

Known gaps and open decisions

  • Settings → Service reasons editor (pos.discountReasons) not built; five fixed reasons are used.
  • Order totals (gross, per rate) show the amount before discounts; only the unbilled figure and the bills include them.
  • Merge leaves each table's order open; closing is separate.
  • Print bill prints one check; the other shares are printed from the order list or Checkout.
  • Tips (row 76) wait for payments and the fiscal module (TSE). Until then no payment or receipt is recorded.
  • Spec and ADR 0036 do not yet describe the discount and split details in this document (whole-line rule, rounding); update before merge.
