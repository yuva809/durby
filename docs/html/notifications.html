Notifications and accounts

Stored updates under the bell, email and browser push per person, and up to five signed-in accounts per browser. Same on app.durby.tech and app.asiamight.site. Decision: ADR 0047. Branch: feature/notifications-accounts (on top of feature/shell-tools). Docs: docs/platform/notifications.md, docs/platform/auth.md → Several accounts.

Bell

[🔔 4]  ← waiting (3) + unread updates (1)
┌ Waiting for you 3 │ Updates 1 ┐
│ ● Your stock request was approved: TRF-2026-000004   18.11. 09:00 │  ← bold + dot = unread
│   Your feedback was dealt with by our team           17.11. 09:00 │
│              [ Mark all as read ]                                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Opening an update marks it read and goes to its page (requests and orders open in their side panel; a driver's delivery opens My Deliveries). Opens on "Updates" when only updates have news. Last 30 shown.

Browser push

Setup (once per environment)

npx web-push generate-vapid-keys

# apps/api/.env (all three or none; keep the private key secret)
WEB_PUSH_PUBLIC_KEY=<Public Key>
WEB_PUSH_PRIVATE_KEY=<Private Key>
WEB_PUSH_SUBJECT=mailto:ops@durby.tech

Restart the api and the worker. Changing the keys later drops every device: people turn push on again.

On a device

Account → Notifications → "Push on this device" → Turn on → the browser asks → allow. Then tick the kinds under Push. The message shows the bell's text; a tap opens the page. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari on macOS; on iPhone/iPad only from the home-screen app.

Several accounts

┌ AW  anna@…            ⇕ ┐   person card menu:
└    AsiaMight · Owner     ┘   Switch organization (if several)
                               Accounts
                                 BW ben@…            ← switch (goes home)
                                 + Add another account   (up to 5)
                               Account
                               Sign out                ← another account takes over
                               Sign out of all accounts

Each account has its own httpOnly cookie (__Secure-durby_rt, _1 … _4), chosen by the X-Durby-Account header. The browser remembers only which email is in which slot. Each tab keeps its own account, so two tabs can show two people. On a shared device, sign out of all when leaving.

Manual

  • Branch sends a request → owner's bell: Updates 1 and an email; approve → the branch user's Updates shows "approved".
  • Warehouse packs with a driver → driver gets an update (and an email by default); the branch gets "on the way".
  • Sell or transfer until an item hits its reorder level → one low-stock update; selling more adds none.
  • Account → Notifications: tick Push for "A branch asks for stock", turn on push, send another request from another browser → a system notification; tap opens the request.
  • Person card → Add another account → sign in as the driver → switch back and forth; open a second tab and switch only there.
  • Sign out (one) → the other account stays; Sign out of all → sign-in page, the list is empty after reload.
