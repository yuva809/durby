Inventory categories and the operations inventory page

Stock items filed by category, categories with description, secondary categories and tile images, and an Inventory page that opens on category tiles. Operations menu only (app.asiamight.site); the default menu is unchanged. Decision: ADR 0048. Branch: feature/asiamight-inventory.

  • A top-level tile counts its secondary categories' items too; a tile opens its items (quantity, value, low badge), with chips for its secondary categories.
  • Export: what is listed, as CSV that Excel opens (UTF-8 with a byte order mark).
  • More: Receive, Adjust, Transfer, History, Items, Warehouses, then Variants, Attributes, Units, Adjustments, Bills of materials, Production (as permitted).
  • Sidebar: Inventory is one item, no submenu; Product Category stays its own item.

Manual

  • Product Category → Add Category: name, description, pick a parent, choose a photo → tile shows the photo on Inventory.
  • Edit a top-level category that has secondary ones: the parent choice is locked.
  • Inventory → Add New Item → pick "African Item › Rice" → the African Item tile count goes up; opening it, the Rice chip lists it.
  • Filter "Low stock" → counts drop to low items; Export → CSV opens in Excel with € and umlauts intact.
  • On a phone (375 px): tiles in two columns, filters stacked, More and Add New Item wrap under the title.
